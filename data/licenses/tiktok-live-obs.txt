The files under src/tiktok-native and tests/media-signing-test.cpp reuse
the request signer, frame signer, SEI encoder, desktop device registration,
cookie/session handling, room lifecycle, topic catalogue, and game-list parser
from that project.

Local modifications add Android requests, synchronous room operations,
cookie-file import, OBS locale integration, and native output lifecycle
management in src/tiktok-live-session.*. No Python or external media proxy
is used. The original repository's LICENSE is retained unchanged.
